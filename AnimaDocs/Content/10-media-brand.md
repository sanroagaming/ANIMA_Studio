## The visual identity

The active mark is the monochrome ribbon A. Use the standalone mark for compact app icons and toolbars; pair it with the ANIMA wordmark for launch visuals and presentation material. Keep the treatment restrained: deep charcoal, neutral whites, clear typography, and a small amount of cyan/violet only when representing the broader motion identity.

## Launch teasers

The bundled MP4s ship with the native app and can be opened from here. Playback is delegated to the Windows default media player; this native documentation app does not host an embedded browser video player.

- **20-second teaser:** concise brand reveal with the “COMING SOON” and “UNRECORDED LABS” end card.
- **45-second teaser:** longer cinematic reveal of code, motion, and runtime concepts.
